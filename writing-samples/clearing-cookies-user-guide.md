# Clearing Cookies – General Browser Guide

This guide covers how to clear cookies in most common browsers. The steps are similar across platforms, but the exact location of settings can vary.

## Steps

1. Click **Settings** in the upper-right corner of your browser. This is usually a three-dot icon, sometimes next to a profile picture.
2. Click **Settings** in the pop-up menu.
3. Look for **Clear browsing history** or **Clear cookies and cache**. Depending on your browser, this might be on the first page of settings, or you may need to search for it.
4. Once the pop-up window appears, select only **cookies and cache** to clear, and set the time range to **All time**. This gives you a clean slate to reload your web pages.

---

> **Note:** Clearing cookies will log you out of any web applications. Make sure to save your work before clearing, and check that you know the passwords to log back into your accounts.
