# Hi, I'm Kili.

I'm a help desk technician and aspiring technical writer based in Portland, Oregon. I spend my days untangling tech problems for real people — and then turning those experiences into clear, useful documentation that helps the next person figure it out on their own.

My background is a bit of a mix: social science, conflict resolution, and several years of front line IT support across schools, government, and tech companies. That combination is actually what draws me to technical writing — I care as much about *why* someone is confused as I do about fixing the problem.

This portfolio is where I'm building and showcasing that work.
